01 / About

About Me

I'm Nicolas, a second-year Software Engineering Co-op student at McGill University, pursuing a minor in Applied Artificial Intelligence.

Alongside my degree, I'm taking an AI Software Development bootcamp through Circuit Stream. My university coursework has given me a foundation in programming with Python and Java, as well as algorithms, data structures, and the mathematical concepts behind computing. I've also been able to apply and expand on that knowledge through the bootcamp, where I've gained hands-on experience with web development and building applications.

What I love most about software engineering is the freedom to turn my ideas into reality. I like understanding how different parts of a system work together and using that knowledge to create something of my own. I'm also particularly interested in AI and the possibilities it opens up for future projects.
